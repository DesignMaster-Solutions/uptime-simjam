    Uptime

    You run a tiny web host out of one rack in a back room. Small businesses pay you by the day to keep their sites up. Keep them up for 60 days.

    • Sites get busy by day and quiet at night. A server above 100% load makes its sites slow and unhappy.
    • Every busy server heats the room. Past 31°C, servers start to die, and every site on a dead server is down.
    • Unhappy sites cancel, and cancellations cost reputation. Reputation brings new sign-ups. At zero, you are done.
    • Click a site chip, then a server, to move it. Rack servers, upgrade them, add cooling, and set your price: cheap grows fast, expensive grows slow.

    Watch for DDoS attacks, sites going viral and heat waves.
